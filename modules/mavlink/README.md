# mavlink: the two MAVLink modules

The modules of `docs/10-mavlink.md`: the vehicle's `mavlink`, ArduSub's only peer, and
the topside's `topside_mavlink`, what QGroundControl talks to. One program,
`hangar-mavlink`, in three modes:

| Mode | Module | |
|---|---|---|
| `hangar-mavlink <mavlink.conf>` | `mavlink` (vehicle) | `mavlink_vehicle.c` |
| `hangar-mavlink --topside <topside_mavlink.conf>` | `topside_mavlink` | `mavlink_topside.c` |
| `hangar-mavlink --neutral <mavlink.conf>` | the vehicle module's `[Actuator] Neutral` command | `mavlink_vehicle.c` |

One program rather than two because the two modules share the framing, the signing,
the configuration grammar and the UDP link; which module a process is comes from its
wiring, as every module's identity does, and the manifest names the mode on its
`Exec` line. The framing (`mav.h/.c`) and SHA-256 (`sha256.h/.c`) are written from the
specification; `mavlink_gen.h` is generated by `tools/mavgen` from the pinned XML
(`tools/mavgen/README.md`: the commit, the licence, what was checked). No pymavlink or
libmavlink at build or run time. GPL-3.0-or-later, C11, libc (libm) and lib/hg only.

Files: `mavlink.module`, `topside_mavlink.module`, the example `mavlink.conf`,
`topside_mavlink.conf`, `ardusub.params`; `tests/fake_ardusub.c`,
`tests/test_mav_frame.c`, `tests/test_mavlink_vehicle.c`, `tests/test_mavlink_topside.c`,
`tests/test_phase1_chain.c`; `mavlink.mk`.

## The vehicle's module

- The ground station ArduSub knows: `SYSID_MYGCS` from `ardusub.params` (else `SysId=`),
  component 190; no `HEARTBEAT`; `MANUAL_CONTROL` at 50 Hz from the actuator gate
  (`lib/hg/hg_gate.h`), neutral when the gate is closed, with docs/10's mapping (`z`
  0..1000, 500 neutral; `s` pitch and `t` roll in the MAVLink 2 extension fields as
  `common.xml` defines them, `enabled_extensions` 3; buttons 0), each axis checked
  finite then clamped. What is sent is `ardusub.manual_control`, referencing
  `ctl.wrench`; neutral names nothing.
- `mavlink.arm` (Allow `safety`): refused unless the newest `safety.state` is fresh and
  says `arming`, ArduSub is disarmed and in MANUAL, and `params` is 1; otherwise
  `MAV_CMD_COMPONENT_ARM_DISARM`, `accepted`, and the `PreArm:`/`Arm:` texts collected
  until 500 ms after the `COMMAND_ACK` into `ardusub.state.arm_reason` (the
  `MAV_RESULT` name when there are none).
- The armed agreement: the window is `safety.state` fresh (within `SafetyTimeout`)
  and `arming` or `outputs_enabled`. When it closes, a disarm goes out and
  `ardusub.state.armed` is 2 (`disarming`) until ArduSub answers; three sends 100 ms
  apart unanswered, or a refused disarm, is reported as armed, a disagreement, and the
  disarm is tried again every second. ArduSub armed outside the window, or out of
  MANUAL while armed, is disarmed at the first message and reported armed for the
  safety module's `autopilot` trigger. Each `ardusub.state` references the
  `safety.state` the gate judged.
- Telemetry by docs/10's table (`ATTITUDE` → `nav.attitude` with the quaternion from
  the Euler angles, `SCALED_IMU` → `nav.imu`, `SCALED_PRESSURE2` → `nav.depth` by
  `(press_abs − SurfacePressure) / (FluidDensity · g)`, `SYS_STATUS` → `power.battery`,
  `SERVO_OUTPUT_RAW` → `thr.out`), rates asked with `MAV_CMD_SET_MESSAGE_INTERVAL` at
  start, every 5 s, and again after a restart. Stamps in hangar's clock by docs/10's
  rule: over a 10 s window of one-second buckets, the smallest arrival-minus-stamp
  offset, reset when `time_boot_ms` goes backwards.
- Parameters: `ardusub.params` read with `PARAM_REQUEST_READ` at start and after a
  restart, each `PARAM_VALUE` published on `ardusub.param`; `params` 1 when every one
  reads as the file, 2 with the first mismatch in the status line. `mavlink.setup`
  (`hangar ardusub-setup`): refused unless `safety.state` is disarmed and ArduSub is,
  else a `PARAM_SET` per line, then the read-back.
- `--neutral`: binds the same port, learns ArduSub's address from its next packet
  (1.5 s; or `Peer=`), sends neutral manual control and a disarm three times, signed.
  On `SIGTERM` the module itself sends neutral and a disarm before it exits.
- Signing: MAVLink 2 signatures on everything sent, every frame received must verify
  (key file, link id 1, timestamps per stream, the one-minute rule) with `Signing=yes`;
  rejected frames are counted in the status line. `Signing=no` for a SITL set up
  without a key.

## The topside's module

- To QGroundControl: system 1, `MAV_TYPE_SUBMARINE`, `MAV_AUTOPILOT_GENERIC`
  (`Autopilot=ardupilot` presents ArduPilot instead); `HEARTBEAT` at 1 Hz from
  `safety.state` (armed from `outputs_enabled`, custom mode 19 MANUAL, `CRITICAL` in
  failsafe) only while the link is up; `ATTITUDE` 10 Hz, `SCALED_PRESSURE2` and
  `VFR_HUD` 5 Hz, `SYS_STATUS` and `BATTERY_STATUS` 2 Hz from the topics; a
  `STATUSTEXT` on every change of `safety.state` with its reason, every refused call,
  `hangar.vehicle.system` leaving nominal, each `ardusub.state.failsafe` text.
- From QGroundControl: `MANUAL_CONTROL` → `pilot.wrench` (normalized, `z` as
  `(z − 500) / 500`, `s`/`t` when their extension bits are set) and `pilot.buttons` at
  50 Hz from the module's own clock, zero when the last input is older than
  `InputTimeout`; buttons by `topside_mavlink.conf` (`arm`, `disarm` once per press,
  `pass` as a bit while held, unmarked ignored); `MAV_CMD_COMPONENT_ARM_DISARM`
  (`COMMAND_LONG` or `COMMAND_INT`) → `safety.arm`/`safety.disarm` with
  `hg_call_start`, `COMMAND_ACK` `ACCEPTED` or `DENIED` with the reason in a
  `STATUSTEXT` when the reply comes; `MAV_CMD_DO_SET_MODE` → `DENIED` and
  "hangar owns modes", `SET_MODE` the text alone (that message has no ack);
  `PARAM_REQUEST_LIST`/`READ` → one parameter `HANGAR`; `PARAM_SET` → the unchanged value
  and a text; `MISSION_REQUEST_LIST` → a count of 0; any other command `DENIED`; the
  rest dropped.

## Deviations from docs/10, and why

1. **Which parameter list and `HEARTBEAT` fields QGroundControl 5.1.5 needs to enable
   the joystick for a generic autopilot is unverified** (docs/10 says it is confirmed
   against the version deployed): this session had no QGC in the loop. `Autopilot=ardupilot`
   is the switch if the generic autopilot leaves the joystick off.
2. **`SET_MODE` gets no `COMMAND_ACK`**: a message is not a command; the `STATUSTEXT`
   says what happened. docs/10 lists both under one row.
3. **`BATTERY_STATUS` from ArduSub is not folded into `power.battery`**; `SYS_STATUS`
   carries the voltage ArduSub measures, and the message is in the tlog.
4. **`pilot.buttons` is `msg/pilot_buttons.msg`** (`u32 pressed`, `u32 reserved`, the
   fields docs/10 gives), named for the topic as the other types are, since a type
   named `buttons` says nothing about whose.
5. **`manual_control.msg`'s comments** said `s` roll and `t` pitch; `common.xml` at the
   pinned commit defines `s` as the pitch-only and `t` as the roll-only axis, so the
   module and the comments follow the XML (the layout and hash are unchanged).
6. **The clock window** has one-second buckets: the minimum over the last ten seconds
   of arrival-minus-stamp, which is docs/10's rule at one-second granularity.
7. **The arm's texts are collected from the send** (not before), and the collection
   window after the ack is 500 ms as docs/10 says.
8. **`Rate=` of the telemetry table is per message, the topics fixed**: a topic's type
   is fixed, so the configuration changes rates (and `From=` picks the pressure
   message), not names.
9. **The HUD rates** (`ATTITUDE` 10 Hz, depth 5 Hz) are the module's choice; docs/10
   gives none. `pilot.wrench` is published from a 20 ms timer rather than on each
   `MANUAL_CONTROL`, so its rate is the declared 50 Hz whatever QGC's send rate is.

## Until the bridge, the recorder, and the vehicle

- `bridge.vehicle.link` / `bridge.topside.link` do not exist: the topside counts the
  link as up while `safety.state` keeps arriving (`LinkTimeout`, 2 s) and stops its
  `HEARTBEAT` otherwise; the vehicle module does not stop `MANUAL_CONTROL` at
  `tether_timeout` (there is no link to time out) and does not subscribe to
  `safety.params`. Marked `until the bridge` in the code and the manifests.
- The topics crossing the tether (`safety.state`, `ardusub.state`, `nav.*`,
  `power.battery`, `pilot.wrench`, `pilot.buttons`, the `.req`/`.rep` of the calls) are
  read through the same-host shim (`lib/hg/NOTES.md` "Phase 1"); the `topside_mavlink`
  caller token comes out as `topside:topside_mavlink` through it, as it will through
  the bridge.
- The raw `.tlog` goes to the path `Tlog=` names, not into a recorder session
  (`recorder.session` does not exist); the sim profile writes one under the vehicle
  root.
- `hangar.vehicle.modules` is not read for the `ardusub` module's instance: SITL is not
  a hangar module on the development machine; `time_boot_ms` going backwards covers
  the restart.
- Signing against SITL is off in the sim profile: MAVProxy's `--out` is not set up
  with a key here. How the key reaches the deployed ArduSub build, and the
  `ExclusiveGroups`/`Neutral` of the `ardusub` module, are confirmed on the vehicle
  (docs/10 says so).
- `hangar check` is the phase 1 subset: `safety.check`. The parameter comparison it
  describes is what `ardusub.state.params` and the module's status line report.

## Tests

- `make check-mav_frame`: CRC-16/MCRF4XX's check value; every `CRC_EXTRA` and length
  against the reference C library's constants; eight byte-exact MAVLink 2 packets
  produced by pymavlink 2.4.50 in a one-off run (checked in as hex; the generator's
  pack functions reproduce them and the decoder reads them back); bad CRC, unknown id,
  truncation, two frames in one datagram, the never-trimmed first byte; SHA-256's
  vectors; a signed packet reproduced bit for bit, verified, replayed (refused), wrong
  key, unsigned on a signed link, a minute behind, the clock following the sender.
- `make check-mavlink_vehicle` (20 s): the module against `fake_ardusub` (a UDP client
  like MAVProxy's `--out`, signing, a parameter table, scripted refusals, a control
  port) with the suite as `safety`: 50 Hz signed neutral; the mismatch named and
  `mavlink.setup` fixing it; the arming sequence and the mapping of every axis;
  non-finite and stale commands neutral; the window closing with `disarming`; a refused
  disarm as a disagreement; ArduSub armed behind hangar's back and out of MANUAL
  disarmed at once; a `PreArm:` refusal with both texts; stamps across a restart,
  rates and parameters asked again; silence; unsigned and wrongly signed frames
  refused by both ends; `--neutral` by the manager and by hand.
- `make check-mavlink_topside` (12 s): the module against the suite as QGroundControl
  and as `safety`: everything under "The topside's module" above, the link down and up.
- `make check-phase1_chain` (15 s): two managers, both roles, the shim, every real
  module, the fake ArduSub, the suite as QGC: joystick → `pilot.wrench` → mux →
  `ctl.wrench` → gate → `MANUAL_CONTROL` at the fake once QGC's arm went through
  `safety.arm` as `topside:topside_mavlink`; disarm → neutral; `safety` killed → neutral
  at ArduSub after **16 ms** (the manager flags the dead writer's topic and the gate
  closes on it; `SafetyTimeout` + one period, 320 ms, is the bound without a manager,
  which `test_hg_gate` covers); `mux` killed → neutral after **21 ms**; both modules
  restarted by the manager, the vehicle disarmed by the window closing.
- `make check-sitl HANGAR_SITL=1`: the chain against the real container
  (`HANGAR_SIM=1 tools/sim/run.sh up`), the fake replaced by SITL on 14560, signing off,
  `mavlink.setup` first, the thrust seen in `thr.out`. Written and wired, **not run in
  this session**: it had no Docker access. It is exercised through the fake only.
